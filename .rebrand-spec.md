## 7. Standing rules that still bind

- Only article content emits `<h1>/<h2>/<h3>`; chrome uses `<p>`/`<span>` +
  `aria-label` on the section.
- No HTML comments (`<!-- -->`) in anything that renders — strip them while
  rethemeing. Frontmatter `//` comments are fine.
- No `mx-auto max-w-*` on body `<p>` in a page; body copy is
  `text-left text-base leading-relaxed`.
- Decorative emoji need their own `aria-hidden="true"` span; no `href="#"`,
  no `onclick=`; `alt` must describe the picture.
- Never write `isActive` by hand — `isActivePath` from `../lib/nav`.
- No `define:vars`, no domain typed by hand, no `Review`/`AggregateRating`/
  `sameAs`/`PostalAddress` in JSON-LD; `pageGraph()` only.
- `BRAND_ADDRESS` derives itself from `BRAND_NAME` — never retype the lines.
