itself from `BRAND_NAME`; never hand-write the address or the email.

## 2. Polarity and palette

**BOOM369 is a DARK brand** — a polarity flip from HI59, which was the
template's first light brand. Every light-glass surface (`#f6fbff`/`#e7f2fd`/
`#d3e7fa`) and navy ink (`#0d2744`/`#04203f`/`#02162c`/`#073059`/`#065a9c`/
`#0a84e0`) and gold (`#fdbc04`/`#ffe27a`/`#c88a02`) belongs to the previous
brand and is gone.

Read off the artwork: the logo is a neon-green **BOOM** + chrome-silver **369**
in a green-to-teal ring on transparent; `banner1` and both slides are a
near-black hall lit by green neon with gold chips and coins; `bg.json` is
`{"mode":"color","color":"#000000"}`.

| role | hex | notes |
|---|---|---|
| **neon accent (primary)** | `#42fb5a` | logo vivid #1. Headings, highlights, borders, glow. |
| neon light | `#8dffa0` | active/hover state, gradient head stop. |
| brand green (mid) | `#04ab3a` | logo vivid #2. Gradient mid-stop, solid fills. |
| brand green (deep) | `#036b26` | gradient end-stop, deep borders. |
| page background | `#000000` | from `bg.json`. |
| card surface — raised | `#0b231b` | banner1 dominant #1. |
| card surface — mid | `#07170f` | default content-card surface. |
| card surface — deep | `#041109` | chrome bars, insets, ticker, navbar, footer. |
| body copy | `#cfe9d7` | = `BRAND_TEXT`. |
| chrome silver (the "369") | `#dadcdc` | secondary labels, second ring colour. |
| gold (sparing) | `#f3c44a` | chips/coins in the art. Accent only, not a second brand hue. |
| border | `rgba(66,251,90,0.28)` → `border-[#42fb5a]/30` |
| glow | `rgba(66,251,90,0.35)` |
| dark ink on neon | `#03150a` | the ONLY text colour allowed on a `#42fb5a`/`#04ab3a` fill. |

Tailwind family: `green-*` / `emerald-*`. The previous brand's was `blue-*` /
`sky-*` + `amber`.

**Contrast, checked:** `#cfe9d7` on `#07170f` ≈ 13.5:1 · on `#0b231b` ≈ 12.0:1 ·
on `#000000` ≈ 15.6:1 · `#42fb5a` on `#07170f` ≈ 14.6:1 · `#03150a` on `#42fb5a`
≈ 13.9:1 and on `#04ab3a` ≈ 6.3:1.
**Do not** put light text on a `#04ab3a` fill (≈ 2.9:1, fails AA) — this brand's
hue is bright, so buttons are *dark ink on a green fill*, never the reverse.

```
primary button  : linear-gradient(in oklab to bottom, #42fb5a, #04ab3a 55%, #036b26)
                  text #03150a, font-black, shadow-[0_0_14px_rgba(66,251,90,0.55)]
secondary button: linear-gradient(in oklab to bottom, #0b231b, #041109)
                  border-[#42fb5a]/60, text #cfe9d7
LINE            : #00B900 — a real third-party colour, never recoloured
```

### Vocabulary

BOOM369 is a **สล็อต / คาสิโนออนไลน์** brand, not a lottery site. Allowed words
are the brief's own: สล็อต, เบทต่ำ, ทุนน้อย, ไม่มีขั้นต่ำ, ค่ายตรง, ถูกลิขสิทธิ์,
ฟรีสปิน, โบนัส, ตัวคูณ, วงล้อ, ตารางจ่าย, ช่วงเดิมพัน, เกมเปิดใหม่, บัญชีเดียว,
เครดิต, โปรโมชั่น, คืนยอดเสีย 0.3%, แนะนำเพื่อน 0.8%, สมัคร 3 ขั้นตอน, Login,
แอดมิน 24 ชั่วโมง, PIN, OTP, ประวัติรายการ, ฝากถอน.

**Banned (previous brands'):** ไฮคลาส, VIP, ส่งตรงจากเว็บแม่, ยอดเข้าบริษัทตรง,
ไม่ผ่านบัญชีม้า, ค่ายเกมพรีเมียม, และคำหวยทั้งหมด (หวย, โพย, เลขเด็ด, แนวทาง,
งวด, อัตราจ่าย).

No licence, regulator, operator, bank, game studio, RTP figure, deposit minimum,
member count or founding year — the brief names none. The only figures are
**0.3% · 0.8% · 100% · 24 ชั่วโมง · 3 ขั้นตอน**. The brief's own hedging ships
with the copy.
